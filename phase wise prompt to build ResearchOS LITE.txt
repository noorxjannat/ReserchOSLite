## 7. Phase Five: Analyze the Seven Research Agents

Understand the seven agent roles defined by the master prompt.

1. Research Navigator: clarifies the research question, domain, scope, keywords, and search strategy.
2. Literature Scout: retrieves, normalizes, deduplicates, and ranks real academic records.
3. Literature Analyst: extracts source-supported research questions, methods, findings, and limitations.
4. Research Synthesizer: compares studies, synthesizes evidence, and identifies agreements and disagreements.
5. Gap Hypothesis Generator: proposes provisional research gaps and explains their evidence and uncertainty.
6. Counterevidence Reviewer: searches for contradictory findings, prior work, and alternative explanations.
7. Research Proposal Writer: generates a preliminary research brief from the human-approved research direction.

The orchestrator must control their execution and state transitions.

For each agent, determine whether it exists, whether its responsibility is correctly separated, whether its input and output schemas are defined, whether it uses real available evidence, whether errors are handled, and whether it has meaningful tests.

Identify opportunities to reuse existing agents rather than rebuilding equivalent functionality.

Never recommend superficial agents that merely call the same generic prompt under different names.

## 18. Environment and deployment rules

Use environment variables for database connections, Supabase configuration, provider credentials, credential-encryption keys, CORS origins, and frontend API URLs.

Never commit actual secrets.

Keep private keys, database credentials, and encryption keys server-side. Only publish configuration explicitly designed for browser use.

Prepare separate deployment configurations for the frontend, FastAPI API, and worker.

Use Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL for persistent production state.

Verify health endpoints, authentication, real academic search, paper saving, workflow execution, source-linked evidence, human approval, brief generation, Markdown export, and persistence after reload.
